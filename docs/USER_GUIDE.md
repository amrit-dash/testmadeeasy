# 📖 User Guide: TestMadeEasy

Welcome to **TestMadeEasy**! This guide will walk you through the core functionalities of the platform, from uploading your first question to downloading a print-ready test paper.

---

## 🚀 1. Getting Started

### Dashboard
When you log in, you will be greeted by the **Dashboard**. Here, you can quickly see:
- Your recent activity.
- The number of questions in your library.
- The number of test papers generated.

Use the sidebar on the left to navigate between different sections of the app.

---

## 📸 2. Uploading Questions

The first step in building a test paper is extracting questions from your materials.

1. Navigate to the **Upload** page from the sidebar.
2. **Choose the Source:** You can either take a clear picture of a question from a textbook/notes or upload an existing image.
3. **Wait for AI Extraction:** The system will process the image. It will detect:
   - Text descriptions.
   - Mathematical/Scientific formulas (which it converts to LaTeX).
   - Any charts, graphs, or diagrams associated with the question.
4. **Review & Save:** The extracted question will appear on your screen. You can edit the text or LaTeX if needed. 
5. Categorize the question by assigning a **Subject**, **Chapter**, **Topic**, and **Difficulty Level**.
6. Click **Save to Library**.

*(Pro Tip: You can also use the **PDF Upload** feature in the admin panel to batch-process multiple pages at once!)*

---

## 📚 3. Managing Your Library

Your **Library** is your personal database. Everything you upload and save goes here.

1. Navigate to the **Library** page.
2. Here, you can search and filter your saved questions:
   - Filter by **Subject** or **Topic**.
   - Sort by **Difficulty**.
3. You can also edit any previously saved questions if you need to update an answer or correct a typo.

---

## 🏛️ 4. Setting Up Your Branding

Before you generate a test paper, set up your institute’s branding so that every paper you create looks professional.

1. Go to the **Branding** page via the sidebar.
2. **Coaching Name & Logo:** Enter the name of your institute and upload a high-resolution logo.
3. **Watermark:** Add a custom watermark text (e.g., "Do Not Copy - XYZ Classes") to protect your material.
4. **Accent Color & Header Style:** Customize the visual appearance of the test paper header to match your brand colors.
5. Click **Save Changes**. All future papers will automatically apply these settings.

---

## 📝 5. Creating a Test Paper

Once you have questions in your library and your branding set up, it's time to build a test!

1. Navigate to **Create Paper**.
2. **Basic Details:** Enter a Title, Subject, Duration, and Total Marks for the test.
3. **Add Instructions:** Add any specific guidelines for the students (e.g., "All questions are compulsory.").
4. **Select Questions:** Browse your personal library or the master database. Click on the questions you want to include to add them to your test paper.
5. **Configure Grading:** Decide if you want to include negative marking and specify the weightage.
6. **Generate:** Click the **Generate Paper** button.

---

## 🖨️ 6. Viewing and Printing Papers

1. Go to the **Papers** section to see a list of all tests you’ve created.
2. Click on a specific paper to view it.
3. You will see a perfectly formatted, print-ready digital view of your test paper, complete with your custom branding.
4. You can also toggle to the **Answer Key** view to see the compiled solutions.
5. Simply use your browser's print functionality (`Ctrl+P` or `Cmd+P`) to save the test as a PDF or print it directly.

---

## ⚙️ 7. Account Management

Need to update your profile or change themes?
- Head to the **Account** page.
- Here you can manage your login details and personalize your experience (Dark/Light mode, UI accent colors).
