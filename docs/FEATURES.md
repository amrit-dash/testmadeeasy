# 🌟 Comprehensive Feature List

**TestMadeEasy** is designed with cutting-edge features to streamline the test paper creation process for teachers and coaching institutes. Here is a deep dive into what makes our platform stand out.

---

## 🤖 1. Advanced AI Processing

### Text & Equation Extraction
Upload any study material — from handwritten notes, printed books, or complex physics PDFs.
- **LaTeX Generation:** Translates complex mathematical equations and symbols precisely into standardized LaTeX format for perfect rendering.
- **Image Cropping & Isolation:** The AI automatically identifies, crops, and processes diagrams, storing them separately so they look perfect on the final test paper.
- **PDF Batch Processing:** Upload an entire PDF, and the platform will read, process, and extract multiple questions simultaneously into your drafts.

---

## 🗂️ 2. Question Bank Management

### Master Questions vs. Personal Library
- **Master Database:** Access thousands of pre-verified questions across various subjects and boards to supplement your tests.
- **Personal Question Bank:** Build your own private database of questions from your unique study materials.

### Smart Categorization
Easily categorize your extracted questions to make them searchable.
- Tag by **Subject**, **Chapter**, and **Topic**.
- Add **Difficulty Levels** (Easy, Medium, Hard).
- Assign **Marks** and **Negative Marks** for grading.

---

## 📄 3. Custom Test Paper Generation

### Dynamic Paper Builder
- Select questions from your library and mix them with master questions.
- Rearrange questions via an intuitive drag-and-drop interface.
- Set global settings like **Total Marks**, **Duration**, and **Test Date**.

### Marking Schemes
- Choose to apply **Negative Marking**.
- Optionally include **Multiple Choice Options** for objective tests or hide them for subjective formats.

---

## 🎨 4. Branding & Personalization

### Institute White-Labeling
The branding page lets you completely personalize the generated PDFs so they represent your coaching institute perfectly.
- **Custom Logos:** Add your high-resolution institute logo.
- **Dynamic Headers:** Choose from multiple header styles (Classic, Modern, etc.) and add your contact info.
- **Accent Colors:** Make your brand color the primary accent for titles and borders on the test paper.
- **Watermarks:** Protect your intellectual property by applying custom text watermarks across the test paper pages.

---

## 🔑 5. Answer Keys & Evaluation

- **Automatic Answer Key Compilation:** Every time you generate a test paper, the system generates a corresponding answer key document.
- **Answer Editing:** Edit and refine the AI-extracted answers for accuracy.
- **System Generated vs. Extracted Answers:** The platform distinguishes between answers it inferred and answers it extracted directly from an image.

---

## 🖥️ 6. Seamless User Experience

- **Responsive Design:** A beautifully crafted UI using Radix UI components and Tailwind CSS ensures the app looks great on both desktops and tablets.
- **Interactive Dashboards:** Visual representations of your library size, generated papers, and recent activity.
- **Theme Support:** Native Light/Dark mode switching for comfortable viewing during late-night paper setting.

---

## 🔒 7. Secure and Scalable

- **Authentication:** Secure login and user management ensures your personal question bank remains entirely private.
- **Cloud Storage:** Images and generated PDFs are stored securely and retrieved at lightning speed.
